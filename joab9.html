The Kenya Civil Report

The Kenya Civil Report (TKCR) is a digital-first Kenyan newsroom founded and owned by Joab James Apopo.

The newsroom is designed to cover Kenyan politics, breaking news, public affairs, governance, county news, business, society, investigations, reports, interviews, opinion, analysis and video journalism.
